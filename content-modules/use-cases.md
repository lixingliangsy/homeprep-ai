# HomePrep AI — Use Cases

## Persona 1 — First-time seller
**Pain:** No idea what actually moves the needle before photos.
**How:** Enter home type + timeline → get a prioritized, budget-aware plan.

## Persona 2 — Busy agent
**Pain:** Every listing needs a different prep pep-talk.
**How:** Generate per-listing plans fast; save history (Pro) for repeat clients.

## Persona 3 — Out-of-town owner
**Pain:** Can't be there to stage.
**How:** Share the checklist with a helper to execute remotely.

## Vertical notes
- Condo: declutter + lighting focus.
- House: curb + kitchen/bath depth.
