# HomePrep AI — Security & Compliance (data handled — lighter tier)

## What we handle
HomePrep AI processes the home type, timeline, budget, and focus you select to generate a prep checklist. Input is preference data only.

## Data handling commitments
- **Minimization (GDPR Art.5):** input is used only to generate your plan; not retained longer than needed. ref: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-5/
- **No training without consent:** we do not train public models on your submissions without explicit consent.
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS. ref: OWASP ASVS V9.

## Compliance posture (honesty rule)
> **We do NOT guarantee** a faster sale or higher price. Staging helps presentation; the market decides outcome. Output is guidance, not real-estate or legal advice.

## Subprocessors
- Hosting: **Vercel**. Payments: **Waffo**. No data sold to third parties.
