# HomePrep AI — How It Works

Home type, timeline, budget, and focus (photos / showings / both).

## Step 2 — Get a prioritized plan
High-impact, low-cost fixes first; scaled to your timeline and budget.

## Step 3 — Execute + save
Work the checklist; save it (Pro) for the next listing.
